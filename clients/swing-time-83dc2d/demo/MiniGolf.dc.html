Two courses, two personalities
Germantown THE CLASSIC
[photo: hole 18 by the dock]
18 winding holes with big elevation changes, 3 waterfalls, and one of the longest holes in the country at over 70 feet. Challenging enough for a grudge match, fun enough for a five-year-old.
70-ft hole 3 waterfalls Bonus 19th hole
Waukesha THE VOYAGE
[photo: lighthouse + pond, upper level]
19 nautical-themed holes on two levels around a pond — lighthouses, anchors, water features, and a few hidden critters if you know where to look. A local favorite for a reason.
