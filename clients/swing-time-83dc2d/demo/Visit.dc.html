Good to know
Do I need a reservation?
Nope — everything is first come, first serve. For groups of 10+ give us a call so we can take care of you properly.
What if the weather turns?
Hours flex with Wisconsin weather. Check the banner at the top of this page or our Facebook for today's status — and if we're busy, we often stay open late.
Do I need my own equipment?
No. Putters, balls, bats, and helmets are all included, and loaner golf clubs are free with any range bucket.
Is Swing Time open in winter?
The Germantown driving range is — our heated bays keep the season going all year. Mini golf and cages return in spring.
