a family thing, from the very first summer
Grandpa built it. We keep it swinging.
Swing Time isn't a chain, a franchise, or an investment portfolio. It's one Wisconsin family, two pieces of land, and a stubborn belief that a summer evening should end with ice cream and a scorecard argument.
[photo: the whole family on the 1st hole — grandparents' era photo layered with today]
two generations & counting

It started the way most good Wisconsin ideas do: with family, a patch of land, and a lot of weekends. Our grandparents believed that a town needs a place where a seven-year-old, a teenager, and a grandpa can all have a genuinely good time — at the same time, for a few dollars. So they built one.

Mini golf came first. Then batting cages, because half the family argued baseball beats golf (this dispute remains unresolved). Then a driving range, because dad needed somewhere to work on his slice. Piece by piece, hole by hole, Swing Time became the place where our neighbors' kids had their birthday parties — and now those kids bring their kids.

Today the second generation runs both locations — Germantown and Waukesha — and not much has changed where it counts. We still walk the course every morning. We still keep the batting cages at a dollar for fifteen pitches, because that's what feels right. We still fix waterfall #2 ourselves. And we're still behind the counter most days, so come say hi.

The joy this place brings people of every age is the whole reason we do it. That was the idea on day one, and it's the idea now.

— the Swing Time family
