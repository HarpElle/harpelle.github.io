New to Beth Messiah?

What to expect on Shabbat

Our Shabbat worship blends Hebrew and English, with music, prayer, and a weekly Torah portion reading that grounds us in the same Scriptures read in synagogues around the world. The service is warm and participatory — expect to stand, sing, and join in responsive readings alongside longtime members and first-time guests alike.

Children are welcomed in the service from the very start, so families can worship together as the gathering begins. During the message, children are dismissed to Shabbat School, where they explore the same Scriptures at a level suited to their age before rejoining their families at the close of the service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from first-time guests

A Messianic congregation is a community of Jewish and Gentile believers who worship Yeshua (Jesus) as the Messiah of Israel while honoring the biblical feasts, Scriptures, and traditions of the Jewish people. At Beth Messiah, that means a service rooted in Torah and Scripture, expressed through Hebrew and English worship, and open to everyone who wants to belong.

We gather for Shabbat worship every Saturday at 10:00 AM at N84W16971 Menomonee Ave, Menomonee Falls, WI 53051. We also have midweek and youth gatherings throughout the month — see our Ministries page for the current schedule.

Not at all. Beth Messiah is made up of both Jewish and Gentile believers, and everyone is welcome to worship, learn, and belong here regardless of background. Many of our members came to us with no prior connection to Jewish tradition and have found a warm home in this community.

Come as you are. Some of our members wear a tallit (prayer shawl) or kippah as an expression of their faith and tradition, but this is entirely optional — there is no dress code, and you will see everything from casual to dressier attire on any given Shabbat.

Children are welcomed in the service alongside their families, then dismissed to Shabbat School during the message so they can explore the same Scriptures at their own level. We also offer midweek children's programming and a youth ministry for older kids and teens.

Plenty of parking is available around the building at N84W16971 Menomonee Ave, Menomonee Falls, WI 53051. If you need handicapped access, the ramp is located on the West side of the building.

Our Shabbat service typically runs 90 minutes to 2 hours. Afterward, we gather for an oneg — a post-service fellowship time with food — where you can relax, meet people, and ask any questions about what you experienced. It's one of our favorite ways to welcome first-time guests.
