Our Story & Mission

About

A New Covenant expression of being Jewish — worshiping the God of Israel, together, in a Jewish way.

A congregation where Jew and Gentile worship Messiah together.

Beth Messiah exists to gather Jewish and Gentile believers into one family under Messiah Yeshua, honoring the God of Israel through worship, Torah, and the fullness of Scripture — while making His salvation known to Jew and Gentile alike.

We do this by:

  • Worshiping God in a Biblical Jewish context.
  • Living a Messianic Jewish lifestyle by embracing the teaching of Messiah Yeshua.
  • Communicating the Good News of Messiah Yeshua to everyone, with a special emphasis to the Jewish people in our community.
  • Caring for those of our Beth Messiah family.
  • Blessing the churches in our community by teaching the Jewish roots of the Messianic faith.
  • Supporting the restoration of Israel to their salvation in Messiah Yeshua.

What is Messianic Judaism?

Messianic Judaism is a movement of Jewish people who believe that Yeshua (Jesus) is the promised Messiah of Israel, while continuing to live as Jews — keeping the biblical calendar, the weekly Torah portion, and the customs that have shaped Jewish life for generations.

It stands apart from both mainstream Judaism, which does not recognize Yeshua as Messiah, and from much of Gentile Christianity, which historically has not retained a Jewish expression of faith. Messianic Judaism holds both together: full faith in Yeshua and a full Jewish identity.

This is not a new invention. It traces its roots to the first-century Jewish followers of Yeshua — the original disciples and the early Jerusalem congregation described in the Scriptures — and to the Jewish believers throughout history who have continued that witness ever since.

“Gentiles who place their faith in Yeshua are grafted into the Jewish olive tree of faith — becoming spiritual sons and daughters of Abraham.”
— Romans 11:17-25
