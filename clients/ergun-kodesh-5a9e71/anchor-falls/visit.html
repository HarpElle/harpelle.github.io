What to wear

Come as you are — suit, jeans, or a jersey. You won't feel out of place.

Your first Sunday

Our services last about an hour and a half and include worship music and a practical, Bible-based message. You'll be greeted at the door, and there's no pressure to do anything but relax and take it all in.

Bring the whole family — we have care and teaching for children of every age, so you can worship with confidence that your kids are safe and loved.

A welcoming shot of the congregation during worship, or families arriving.

Frequently asked questions

Sunday School is available for all children K3 – 6th grade in the classrooms on the lower level. Children are prompted to exit the chapel for Sunday School immediately following worship. Nursery care is also available for young children throughout the duration of our service.

There is plenty of parking around our building. Handicapped parking is located by the main entrance on the East side of the building, but the ramp access is located on the West side.

We want you to feel comfortable, whatever that means for you. Whether it's a suit or jeans or a jersey, you won't feel out of place.

There are several ways to connect. First, sign up for our weekly newsletter at the bottom of this page. You can also follow us on social media @AnchorFallsChurch. We also have Equip Groups — Bible study groups that meet weekly and discuss a variety of topics. Email Mark Holdmann at MarkHoldmann@anchorfalls.org for more information.
