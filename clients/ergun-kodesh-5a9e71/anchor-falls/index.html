Welcome Home

A place for your whole family to grow in faith

Anchor Falls Church in Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin, is a cross-generational, interdenominational, Bible-believing congregation passionate about knowing God. In short, we're a community where your whole family can grow in faith and develop lasting relationships.

We would love to have you join us for an upcoming service. If you do not have a church home, we sincerely hope you consider joining the Anchor Falls Church family.
