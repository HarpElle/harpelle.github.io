For Every Generation

Ministries

From our littlest ones to our seasoned saints, there's a place for every member of your family to grow, belong, and serve.

Children's Sunday School classroom.
Infant – 6th Grade

child_careChildren's Ministry

From the nursery through 6th grade, children explore the Bible in ways they can understand and enjoy. Nursery care is available throughout the entire service for our youngest ones, and K3–6th grade children are dismissed downstairs for Sunday School immediately following worship.

Led by Wendy Weaver (Sunday School), Sandy Dearing (POGS Midweek), Helen Dachs (Nursery)

Youth night with The Crew.
7th – 12th Grade

groupsThe Crew

The Crew is where 7th–12th graders connect through worship, teaching, small groups, and a whole lot of fun. It is a welcoming place for students to be known, to wrestle honestly with faith, and to grow into who God made them to be.

Led by Griffin & Maddy Schultz, Youth Directors

Adults around a living-room table, coffee and open Bibles.
Adults 18+

menu_bookEquip Groups

Equip Groups are Bible study groups for adults 18 and older that meet weekly in homes throughout the community. They discuss a variety of topics and are the best next step for building relationships and growing in your faith beyond Sunday morning.

Led by Mark Holdmann, Pastor

Find an Equip Group
