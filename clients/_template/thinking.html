The market you’re in

How people find you Search is local and mobile Most first visits arrive from a phone via "bowling near me." Today that search lands on a page that takes eight seconds to load and hides the hours two clicks deep.
Who decides Parents plan, kids choose Birthday parties and league sign-ups are decided by parents comparing two or three options in one sitting. The clearest prices and easiest booking usually win.
What sets you apart Forty years is the story The chains can outspend you, but they can’t out-story you. Three generations behind the counter is the one thing no competitor can copy.

Who you’re up against

An honest read — what each one does well, and what we’d deliberately do differently.

The chain across town Does well: Online booking that works, consistent photos, fast site. We’d differ: Their site could belong to any city. Yours should feel like it belongs to your neighborhood.
The entertainment megaplex Does well: One roof, many attractions — strong for big group events. We’d differ: They compete on volume; we’d compete on warmth, leagues, and regulars who are known by name.
Your current website Does well: The essentials exist: address, phone, a menu PDF. We’d differ: It answers questions reluctantly. The new site should answer them before they’re asked.

What your customers expect

Hours and prices without hunting — the #1 reason people bounce to a competitor’s site

Real photos of the real place — stock photography reads as hiding something
